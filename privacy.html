Last updated: August 18, 2026

paint2K is a pixel-art drawing app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

paint2K does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We have no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Your images

Everything you draw, and any photo you import to trace or draw over, stays on your device. Images never leave your device or get sent to us. When you choose to export a picture, the app asks iOS for permission to save it to your Photos library — that permission is used only for the save you requested.

In-app purchases

paint2K Plus (subscription and lifetime unlock) is sold and processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles your payment details and purchase history under its own privacy policy; we never see or receive your payment information. The app only learns whether your purchase is currently active, so it can unlock the paid features.

Third parties

paint2K does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any to share.

Children's privacy

paint2K does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date above.

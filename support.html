Restoring a purchase

If paint2K Plus isn't unlocked after buying it on another device, or after reinstalling the app, open the Plus panel and tap Restore. The panel appears when you use a Plus feature: File > Import Photo, or the reference button in the toolbox.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your device: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > paint2K. The monthly plan renews unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Importing a photo

File > Import Photo turns a picture from your library into pixel art you can draw over. It's part of paint2K Plus, along with tracing over a reference image.

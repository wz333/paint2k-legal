Common questions

Restoring a purchase

If paint2K Plus isn't unlocked after buying it on another device, or after reinstalling the app, open Settings inside the app and use Restore Purchases.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your device: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > paint2K.

Importing a photo

File > Import Photo turns a picture from your library into pixel art you can draw over. It's part of paint2K Plus.
